The Process Behind These Three Pages
01
Collect

Bank, card, and platform statements, plus receipts.

02
Enter

Every transaction typed in with date, payee, and amount.

03
Categorize

Each line filed to the chart of accounts. Card one, above.

04
Reconcile

Matched to the bank statement until the gap is $0.00. Card two.

05
Report

A Profit & Loss the owner can actually read. Card three.

06
Flag

Anything unclear goes on a question list — never a guess.

Every register above has at least one line that isn't what it looks like — a platform deposit, an owner's draw, a card payment. Miscoding any one of them inflates the numbers a client uses to make real decisions. Catching them, every time, is what separates data entry from bookkeeping.

How These Systems Work
01

A new email, an overdue invoice, a form submission — something happens first.

02
Route & Act

A router decides which path it is, then AI drafts the part that needs judgment.

03
Log & Notify

Every run writes itself back to a sheet or CRM and pings whoever needs to know.
